# prelegal

A platform for drafting common legal agreements.

> **Status: early work in progress.** The repository is being scaffolded — the
> sections marked _TBD_ below will be filled in as the codebase lands.

## Overview

prelegal helps you produce first drafts of the agreements that come up
repeatedly in running a business — NDAs, contractor and consulting agreements,
service agreements, and similar — by turning a short set of answers about the
deal into a structured, reviewable document.

The goal is to remove the blank-page problem and the copy-paste-from-an-old-
contract habit, and to give a consistent starting point that a lawyer can review
quickly.

## Who it's for

Founders, operators, and small legal teams who need a reasonable first draft
fast, and who will still have a qualified attorney review anything that matters.
